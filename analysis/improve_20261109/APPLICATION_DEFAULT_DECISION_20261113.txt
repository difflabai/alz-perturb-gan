Why this objective fits the intended work
----------------------------------------
The intended research use needs realistic gene detection and cellular
variation, not only a mean expression vector. Original6-fit evidence costs
about .057% in profile RMSE against the current default while reducing
mean-log and detection errors by about20% and23%, with better variance and
projected distribution scores. Protecting every seed-detectable RMSE change
would overweight computational repeatability relative to usefulness.
The .5% margin was declared before new extra-fold scores, not chosen to fit
them. All target and distribution guards in PROTOCOL_DISTRIBUTION_CONFIRM_
20261113.txt still bind. Seed SE describes computational stability; it does
not supply independent biological uncertainty from37 reused donors.

Useful development applications

- Finite, depth-matched synthetic donor bags for exploratory simulations and
