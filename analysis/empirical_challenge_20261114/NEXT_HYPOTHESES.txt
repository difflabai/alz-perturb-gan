Prospective next-study ideas; planning only, not launched
========================================================
The active empirical_challenge_20261114 fixed screen/confirmation retains its
original gates and candidate set. These ideas were considered with the
application_default_review subagent without inspecting new candidate scores.
They do not make an ineligible current arm eligible or authorize a weight grid.

Priority1: one fixed50/50 whole-bag empirical/GAN mixture. Half the generated
patients would use stage-blind training-donor resampling; half the frozen
count-shape GAN. Preserve valid model snapshot/stage allocation and exclude
each training target from empirical sources. This is a semiparametric model:
it reuses observed cells and would support population simulation, not establish
learned patient-vector validity. It could combine empirical covariance/tails
with improved model gene marginals. A later protocol must freeze component
sampling, calibration replay, streams, resource limits, exact paired references
and confirmation before generation. No mixture-weight search. Reject if either
proper-score target or protected gene/profile metrics fails prospective gates.

Priority2: an independent empirical distribution of TRAIN-donor log-dispersion
factors fitted with frozen expected nonzero probabilities, centered and bounded
by one fixed procedure, followed by pooled train-only joint calibration.
This tests missing donor variation in count noise without identifying latent
coordinates as biology. It can absorb composition/depth/technical effects and
must be described as synthetic variability. No offset-strength grid. Detection
gains without SD and combined proper-score gains falsify the proposed benefit.

Do not restart the old g.dispz learned linear donor head: its earlier extra-fold
gain did not confirm. Existing randproj/split/hier decoder variants also failed
their original fixed gates. Direct PCA interval recalibration belongs to a
separate range-estimation benchmark because it need not improve generated cells.
A new default still requires matched superiority, confirmation, full artifact
provenance, actual public CLI proof and a commit. Neither proposed success alone
validates patient representations; reconstruction and repeatability need separate
prospective evidence. Sealed/external donors remain untouched.
