This is the second mechanism recorded before hybrid outcomes in commit7a9eae9:

One fixed factor32 recipe
-------------------------
Use frozen pbulk20k weights, current within-bag contraction alpha=.5, six
snapshot states and stagehalf allocation, M32/K256/64PC cells. Do not retrain.
First regenerate original32/current32/empirical32 on fresh matched streams.

Replay this fit's current32 rounded TRAIN-only bias and gene-dispersion
coefficients on separately loaded original weights. For each TRAIN donor d:
use its fixed M32/K256 model means and dispersions, generated with the same
model streams/plans as the fresh current32 reference. Cache ONE donor's
tensors in RAM, then discard them. Let target_d be its observed nonzero
fraction averaged uniformly over its cells and the2000 genes. Let F_d(u)
be the expected NB nonzero probability averaged over those generated bags,
cells and genes under theta*exp(u), with means fixed.

Fit u_d in[-1,1] with exactly12 bisection steps. Targets at/below F_d(-1)
receive-1; targets at/above F_d(1) receive+1. Otherwise bisect, move the lower
endpoint when F(mid)<target and upper otherwise, then use the final bracket
midpoint. Record endpoints, clipping, residual and all TRAIN donor IDs.
No held-out nonzero/profile/count statistic enters this fit.

Normalize h_d = u_d + log(mean_j exp(-u_j)). Then h lies in[-2,2] and
mean_j exp(-h_j)=1 (up to rounding). Uniform independent factor draws thus
preserve the population NB excess-variance multiplier BEFORE pooled
recalibration, conditional on a fixed mean/dispersion. This protects a noise
moment; it does not guarantee final logvariance or PC-spread guards pass.
Do not arithmetic-center logs, rescale strength, fit a latent regression,
or search a bound/mixture/strength grid. The same uniformly weighted TRAIN
factor law applies to all targets. Never assign a target its personally
fitted factor or condition selection on held-out observations.

Reload fresh original weights. Install theta*exp(h) INSIDE the outer pooled
ZC wrapper and apply the fixed cell-shape contraction. Refit the existing
unchanged three-step joint TRAIN-only bias/nonzero calibration. Precompute
factor assignments by target identity and bag index on a separate private
stream and reuse them across every calibration pass and final generation.
Factor draws do not consume the original global/latent/cell/count/library
streams. Sampled factors are empirical simulation parameters, potentially
absorbing composition, depth and technical effects as well as biology.
