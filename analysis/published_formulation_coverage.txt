The follow-up retains a 20,000-update external cap while leaving continuous-policy total_steps=None, and supplies the documented EMA critic. E22/Atlas still reject conditional table-row semantics with their native row controls enabled. Routed E22 requires an explicit RoutedRows contract. No controls were disabled to force construction.

A KA2/K3P trial needs a faithful critic EMA update and checkpoint plus preset-specific noise/averaging/serving behavior. BCAP/Adam is another currently untested count-adapter preset. Successful initialization is insufficient for native admission or a quality claim.

MoG, DDGAN and AE/VAE model families are not covered by these policy-preset construction checks. Any new family must retain the original count contract, fitting/calibration/evaluation separation, fixed endpoint, exact source binding and full checkpoint/score audit.
