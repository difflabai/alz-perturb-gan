Choice made with application_default_review
-----------------------------------------
The frozen CountGenerator.rates samples independent per-gene Gamma rates
conditional on decoder mu/theta. Failed split/hier decoders changed MU/state
paths while retaining that head. Test joint count dependence separately from
the scalar MU/precision laws already rejected. A high-dimensional donor
residual transport from24-25 donors is not the next probe.

For a fixed cell and gene g in a fixed group m, consider
 U_g = H_m(a*theta_g) + I_g,
 H_m(t)-H_m(s) ~ Gamma(t-s,rate1), independent increments,
 I_g ~ Gamma((1-a)*theta_g,rate1), independent of H and other I,
 rate_g = mu_g/theta_g * U_g; count_g|rate_g ~ Poisson(rate_g).
Each H_m is fresh and independent for EVERY CELL, not shared across cells
or bags. Groups are independent. At a=.5, U_g has Gamma(theta_g,rate1)
marginal, and within-group off-diagonal conditional count covariance is
 a*mu_i*mu_j*min(theta_i,theta_j)/(theta_i*theta_j).
The conditional raw-count covariance diagonal is unchanged. This adds only
nonnegative conditional raw-count dependence; it cannot remove MU covariance
or represent arbitrary signed dependence. Exact statements are mathematical
distribution statements, not byte identity or exact finite-precision laws.

This is our proposed model diagnostic, not a published validated patient
model. Gamma independent-increment definition: Regazzini/Lijoi/Pruenster2003,
https://didattica.unibocconi.it/mypage/dwload.php?nomefile=AnnStatist200320170210164836.pdf
Joint expression and Poisson measurement can be distinguished in coexpression
models; sequencing depth matters (Su etal2023, CS-CORE):
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-40503-7
These sources motivate/check the construction, not its ALZ performance.

Gamma numerical requirements
----------------------------
For a=.5 sort exact float64 a*theta within each group. Draw every strictly
positive time increment in float64 and accumulate the independent increments
within that group. Exact tied times have deterministic zero increments.
Never floor, omit or merge a strictly positive shape/time increment. Add the
independent float64 Gamma((1-a)*theta,1), scale by mu/theta, then cast rates
to the original mean dtype before the frozen explicit count-stream Poisson.
Validate positive finite theta, nonnegative finite mu/rates/counts throughout.

Torch Gamma sampling clamps numerical underflow draws to dtype.tiny, including
in the original head. Retain those sampled values and explicitly count/report
tiny hits in BOTH shared increments and independent components, minimum
positive shapes/times/samples and any final-cast underflow.
For each affected output bound the extra rate mass by
 (group_size+1)*float64.tiny*mu/theta; retain maximum and summed rate-error
bounds, requiring maximum<1e-12 and sum across the full diagnostic<1e-8.
Do not call the resulting machine marginals exact. Before any data, synthetic
checks must cover ties/tiny increments, nonfinite rejection, Gamma marginal
moments and covariance at moderate shapes, and tiny-hit frequencies against
the analytic Gamma CDF mass at float64.tiny. Any material numerical failure
aborts this diagnostic; no shape-floor rescue or kernel/precision search.
