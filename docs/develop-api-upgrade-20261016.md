## Late TERM cancellation

The old wrapper snapshots GOT_TERM before launching the probe. A wrapper-only TERM during the probe could therefore leave a clean terminal receipt and an incomplete wrapper exit.

The wrapper now accepts TERM throughout probing and hashing. The probe writes an exclusive `post_run_probe_observation.json` with `publication=provisional` and no verdict. After all slow work, the wrapper closes cancellation acceptance with `trap '' TERM`, snapshots the final flag, and invokes a short hash-verified finalizer. Only that finalizer writes the exclusive terminal `post_run_receipt.json`; all accepted TERM signals make it incomplete (exit 8 for clean residue). Signals after this documented commit boundary concern an already finalized chain. Residue attention still exits 6. Existing standalone probe calls retain their direct-finalization behavior.

Deterministic CPU regressions use barriers to send wrapper-only TERM (a) after probe launch and (b) after provisional observation creation. Both stop cases require the final receipt and wrapper to agree on incomplete/8; matching no-TERM controls require complete/0. Real dry-run supervisor and real probe/finalizer are exercised. Fake-probe fixtures were updated to the new two-phase protocol. No real training process is signalled by these tests.
